## 📍 Overview

ReplyFlow is an interactive web-based application that simulates a WhatsApp Business automation system. It allows users to experience how automated replies, lead tracking, and revenue generation work together in a real-world business scenario.
The project focuses on solving a common problem faced by businesses — delayed or inconsistent responses to customer inquiries. Through its live chat simulator, ReplyFlow instantly processes user input, identifies intent using keyword-based logic, and generates appropriate automated responses. This creates a seamless and engaging experience that closely mimics real-time customer support systems.
In addition to chat automation, the platform dynamically updates key business metrics such as active leads and revenue. As users interact with the system, they can observe how conversations evolve into conversions, providing a clear understanding of customer journey and business growth.
The application also emphasizes user experience through smooth animations, responsive design, and intuitive UI components. It demonstrates strong frontend development skills while also showcasing logical flow, state management, and real-time interaction handling.
Overall, ReplyFlow is not just a UI project — it is a concept-driven simulation that reflects how modern businesses can leverage automation and intelligent systems to scale customer engagement efficiently.
