 **StudyFlow is a smart and intuitive study assistant designed to help students move beyond traditional cramming and start learning in a more effective and scientific way. The platform focuses on improving memory retention, understanding concepts clearly, and optimizing study time using proven techniques like active recall and spaced repetition.
Instead of overwhelming students with complex features, StudyFlow provides a clean and minimal interface where users can upload their study material, extract key ideas, test themselves through questions, and review content at the right time. It transforms passive studying into an interactive and engaging process, helping students retain information longer and perform better in exams.**

## 📍 Overview

StudyFlow is a web-based application that simplifies the entire learning process by breaking it into three essential steps: understanding, testing, and reviewing. The platform allows users to upload various types of study materials such as PDFs, images, or text files, which are then processed to extract meaningful concepts and summaries.
Once the content is analyzed, the system generates key ideas and organizes them in a structured format, making it easier for students to grasp the main concepts. It then provides a self-assessment feature through automatically generated questions, encouraging active recall — a highly effective learning technique.
One of the most important features of StudyFlow is its review system, which uses a simplified spaced repetition approach. Based on the user’s performance, the system schedules future revisions at optimal intervals, ensuring that information is reinforced before it is forgotten.
Additionally, the platform tracks user progress through metrics such as mastered topics, retention percentage, and daily streaks. This not only motivates users but also provides a clear picture of their learning journey.
Overall, StudyFlow is designed with a strong focus on usability, clarity, and effectiveness. It demonstrates the integration of logical thinking, user-centered design, and educational principles to create a tool that genuinely enhances the way students learn.

## 🌟 Features

- **Upload study material (PDF, images, text files).**
- **Automatic extraction of key concepts and main ideas.**
- **Smart quiz generation for self-testing.**
- **Answer reveal system for better understanding.**
- **Spaced repetition-based review system.**
- **Progress tracking (retention %, mastered topics).**
- **Daily study streak tracking.**
- **Save sessions and review later.**
- **Clean, responsive, and user-friendly interface.**
